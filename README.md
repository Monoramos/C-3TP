# C-3TP


pin ideas and categories:


Character & Mascot: original creatures, cute animals, kawaii figures. The backbone of most pin brands.
Pop Culture & Fandom: fan art and references to games, shows, and movies (licensing matters here if you sell).
Text & Humor: sarcastic phrases, memes, puns, and wordplay. Cheap to design and often top sellers.
Nature & Botanical: plants, mushrooms, flowers, crystals, insects, moths.
Travel & Places: national parks, city skylines, landmarks, retro souvenir badge styles.
Food & Drink: coffee, boba, sushi, snacks with faces, cocktails.
Space & Cosmic: planets, astronauts, moons, constellations. Glow and glitter effects shine here.
Dark & Occult: skulls, tarot cards, witchy imagery, gothic and spooky designs.
Hobby & Profession: cameras, tools, gaming controllers, coding, nurse/teacher pins.
Cause & Identity: pride, awareness ribbons, activism, mental health messages.
